Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from The Prayer App, you can do so using one of the methods below.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. All your saved prayers, decrees, preferences, and account information will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account

Choose the method that works best for you:

1 Delete from Within the App (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly through the app:

  1. Open The Prayer App on your device
  2. Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top corner
  3. Select "Account" from the menu
  4. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm your decision when prompted

Your account and all associated data will be deleted immediately.

2 Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the app or are unable to delete your account through the app, you can request account deletion by email:

  1. Send an email from the same email address you used to register your account
  2. Include the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. In the body, confirm that you wish to permanently delete your account
support@trytheprayerapp.com

Email requests are processed within 7 business days after we verify ownership of the account. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following personal data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your profile information (name, email, account credentials)
  • Personal preferences and settings
  • Saved prayers and decrees linked to your account
  • Audio reflections and saved content
  • Prayer history associated with your account

Anonymized, non-personal data such as aggregated usage statistics may be retained to help us improve the app experience for all users. This data cannot be used to identify you.

Note about subscriptions: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel any active subscriptions. Please cancel your subscription through your device's app store before or after deleting your account to avoid future charges.

Questions?

If you have any questions about account deletion or need assistance, please contact us:

support@trytheprayerapp.com
